# 11 CFR 110.20 - Foreign National Prohibition

CITE-REG-110.20

## Overview
Federal law prohibits foreign nationals from making contributions or expenditures in connection with any U.S. election.

## Who is a Foreign National?
- A foreign government or political party
- An individual who is not a U.S. citizen AND not a lawful permanent resident (green card holder)
- A foreign corporation, partnership, or association
- Any entity organized under foreign laws with principal place of business outside the U.S.

## Prohibited Activities

### Contributions
Foreign nationals may NOT:
- Make any contribution to any political committee
- Make any donation to any political party
- Make any expenditure (independent or otherwise)
- Make any disbursement for electioneering communications

### Solicitation
U.S. persons may NOT:
- Solicit contributions from foreign nationals
- Accept contributions from foreign nationals
- Receive contributions from foreign nationals
- Help foreign nationals make contributions

## Application to Campaign Chatbots

### What BrandonBot MUST DO:
1. NOT process donations from foreign nationals
2. NOT collect detailed donor information (that's for proper FEC-compliant systems)
3. Refer donation interest to proper channels with human verification
4. NOT solicit contributions without proper verification systems

### Safe Practice:
- Do not process any donations through the chatbot
- Redirect all donation interest to official, FEC-compliant donation platforms
- Log donation interest for human follow-up only

## Penalties
- Civil: Up to $25,000+ per violation
- Criminal: Willful violations may result in imprisonment
