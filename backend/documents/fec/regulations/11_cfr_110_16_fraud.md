# 11 CFR 110.16 - Fraudulent Misrepresentation

CITE-REG-110.16

## Overview
Prohibits fraudulent misrepresentation of campaign authority and deceptive practices in political communications.

## Prohibited Conduct

### Misrepresentation of Authority
No person shall fraudulently misrepresent themselves as:
- Speaking, writing, or acting on behalf of a candidate
- Speaking, writing, or acting on behalf of a political party

### Deceptive Fundraising
Prohibited activities include:
- Fraudulently soliciting contributions using another's name
- Misrepresenting affiliation with candidates or committees
- Creating confusion about the source of communications

## Application to AI/Chatbots
When operating a chatbot for a political campaign:
1. Must clearly identify as an automated system
2. Cannot claim to be the candidate
3. Cannot claim authority not granted
4. Must not deceive users about the nature of communication

## Penalties
- Civil penalties under FECA
- Possible criminal prosecution for willful violations
- FEC enforcement proceedings

## Volunteer Context
Even volunteer-operated systems must:
- Accurately represent their nature (AI/automated)
- Not claim false authority
- Clearly state connection to campaign
