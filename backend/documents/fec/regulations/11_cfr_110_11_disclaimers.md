# 11 CFR 110.11 - Disclaimer Requirements

CITE-REG-110.11

## Overview
Federal regulations require disclaimers on all public communications made by political committees and on certain communications by other persons.

## Key Requirements

### General Disclaimer
Every public communication made by a political committee must contain a disclaimer stating:
1. Who paid for the communication
2. Whether it was authorized by any candidate or candidate's campaign

### Format Requirements
- Must be "clear and conspicuous"
- In print: contained in a box set apart from content
- Minimum 12-point font for print up to 24"x36"
- Must have reasonable color contrast

### Specific Disclaimer Texts

**Authorized by Candidate:**
"Paid for by [Committee Name]"

**Authorized but not financed by Candidate:**
"Paid for by [PAC Name] and authorized by [Candidate Committee]"

**Not Authorized:**
"Paid for by [Organization] ([website/address/phone]) and not authorized by any candidate or candidate's committee."

## Digital/Online Communications
- Website and email communications require disclaimers
- Social media ads placed for a fee require disclaimers
- Must be clearly visible to readers

## Volunteer Project Exception
Volunteer activities that do not involve payment or are not "public communications" may have reduced disclaimer requirements, but AI/bot disclosure is still required under state laws.
